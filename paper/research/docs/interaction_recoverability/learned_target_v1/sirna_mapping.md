# Mapping to measured siRNA contrasts

Status: existing repository evidence inspected; no biological fitting, new assay acquisition, graph architecture or wet-lab experiment was performed. The synthetic pilot tests a statistical procedure under stipulated calibration. It is not biological validation.

A concrete *possible* estimand is the change in an original-scale knockdown endpoint due jointly to a specified guide-position-6 2'-O-methyl modification and a specified guide-position-10 2'-fluoro modification, on one fixed sequence, passenger strand, linkage pattern, delivery formulation, dose, time and cell system. Denote the independently measured reference/single/single/joint means by mu00, mu10, mu01, mu11. The contrast is `mu11-mu10-mu01+mu00`. These example modifications do not designate an audited, experimentally complete quartet: none is claimed available. Positions, chemistry and reference sugar states would have to be fixed before selecting rows or observing the joint endpoint.

The statistical model's `e3,e4` are additional, component-selective **unit** anchors, not the above joint modification. Ordinary chemical edit labels, a dose covariate or a graph embedding do not demonstrate component selectivity, unit effect, Gaussian latent calibration, or the known h_delta. Their biological validity is currently unsupported. If those assumptions cannot be calibrated, this theorem is not applicable even when a measured four-state contrast exists.

All numerical counts in this table are retained **historical audit results**, read from the linked reports; they were not recomputed or silently updated during this pilot. P3/P4 negative findings, APP/construct/isoform uncertainty, delivery stereochemistry, repeated structures and provenance caveats remain preserved. The new run's preservation hashes protect these reports and the user evidence file.

There are three separate questions: estimating a measured biological contrast, predicting an unmeasured contrast from measured source laws under an identified structural model, and explaining a frozen predictor. The present pilot addresses the second synthetically. A frozen-GNN interaction would instead be four model queries, with all descriptors and stereochemistry rebuilt after each feasible edit; its agreement with those queries establishes fidelity to that predictor. It does not establish agreement with mu11 or a molecular mechanism. No GNN is needed by the present estimator, and none was inserted or trained.

Any later biological study must first identify actual eligible backgrounds and independent action groups, preserve assay/batch units in splits, and either validate the anchor/warp assumptions or change the mathematical problem explicitly. A larger synthetic run cannot repair missing biological measurements or calibrations.
