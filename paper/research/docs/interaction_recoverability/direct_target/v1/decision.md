**Unresolved.** The bounded proof-and-construction attempt is complete. It produced an exact direct-target identity and a feasible conservative estimator, but not a sharp attainable rate with both profiles and all four coefficients unknown.

**PROVED HERE — strongest completed result.** For every original-class parameter and every finite integer K, the target equals the integrated private-anchor differences with signed weights `W_(j,K)(x)=sum_(k=1)^K K_j(x+k)`, plus an explicitly bounded signed second-difference remainder. A compact taper permits direct empirical-quantile evaluation. The finite-sample bound in [estimator.md](estimator.md) separately accounts for sampling, shared-reference covariance, all four coefficient errors, telescoping, taper tails and arithmetic. No unbounded nonlinear remainder or unknown nuisance radius is hidden in that conservative bound.

The available source-only coefficient construction and explicit tuning schedule give `MSE=O_delta(exp[-.5 sqrt(log n_*)])` at fixed positive delta. This retains the old sufficient slow order; the integrated sampling calculation is sharper at fixed truncation but proves no overall rate improvement.

**STANDARD RESULT APPLIED — closest equivalence.** The rearrangement is the adjoint of the finite telescoping inverse applied to the target kernel, followed by a tapered L-statistic. Functional inverse estimation, empirical-quantile integration and regularization are established operations. The source comparison in [novelty.md](novelty.md) does not support claiming them as a distinct method.

**OPEN — the single unresolved sharp inequality.** For fixed positive delta and positive pi, it remains unproved or unrefuted that a finite C_(delta,pi) satisfies

`|I(eta)-I(eta')| <= C_(delta,pi) [sum_(a=0)^4 pi_a H(P_eta,a,P_eta',a)^2]^(1/2)`

uniformly for sufficiently close pairs in the original class. The complete-experiment subclass lower modulus is linear in epsilon with the inverse-delta penalty; the available global upper modulus is only stretched-log. A proof of this inequality alone would still need an attainable statistical construction; a counterexample would require an appropriate stronger lower bound and slower matching estimator. The present attempt supplies neither, so it does not choose a replacement project.

**PROVED HERE — precise obstruction, narrower than the objective.** For fractional alpha shifts, the naive infinite anchor weight has a nonzero periodic negative-tail limit. At admissible exponential profiles its e3 influence has infinite variance. This invalidates an unqualified root-N argument for that particular untruncated formula. It does not establish impossibility for all five laws: e1/e2 contributions and coefficient corrections could supply a different finite-variance representation. The fixed-parameter and global questions remain distinct.
