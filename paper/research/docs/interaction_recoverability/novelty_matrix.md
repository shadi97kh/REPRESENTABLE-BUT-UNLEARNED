# Source comparisons and novelty decision

**Decision: incremental-known for the completed restricted result.** The precise completed distinction is a finite-action, finite-sample, contrast-specific calculation in a *known-warp* two-exponential model, with an explicit weak-separation parameter. The calculation is useful evidence, but its mechanism reduces to likelihood comparison plus minimum-distance inversion. It does not establish a new general ML/statistical method.

The cutoff is September 12, 2026. Primary PDFs and hashes are retained in the [source manifest](../../../../runs/interaction_recoverability/prep-20260912-082154/sources/manifest.json). Comparisons below report the source's result, not a claim that its theorem applies to this experiment. The original DOCX is starting evidence; its equation images were inspected and its table was independently reproduced. The separate supplied diagnostic script was not present at the last file search, so its source code has not been audited or executed.

For precision, the COLT Theorem 2 bound contains terms of the following form in its notation:

\[
R_{\rm test}\lesssim_*
r^4\epsilon_{11}^2+\alpha r^2(\sigma_{r+1}^*)^2+
(\operatorname{tail}_1^*(r))^2+
\alpha\frac{r^6\epsilon_{11}^4+\epsilon_{\rm trn}^4+(\operatorname{tail}_2^*(r))^2}{(\sigma_r^*)^2}.
\]

Its conditioning requirement and training-coverage constants cannot be discarded when comparing this to a \(\delta^{-2}\) penalty. Our recovered two-quantile matrix simply provides a particularly explicit singular-value scale. No claim of a better COLT rate is supported.

The adjacent search covered named weak-identification literature, functional inverse problems, Hellinger moduli, optimal recovery and recent Le Cam duality work. It found strong direct precedents rather than evidence of absence. The DExtrI preprint's future-work statement does not establish global novelty. The selected protected contrast avoids a weak direction through an exact known identity, which is already the premise of standard functional estimability.

**OPEN — potentially substantive distinction.** A useful new result would need unknown nuisance mechanisms and a verifiable observation/design condition under which a specific unseen contrast is recoverable more stably than all components, with a rate and feasible estimator beyond the applicable inverse-functional/likelihood/completion result. Neither that condition nor such an estimator is established here. No training is recommended merely to seek an empirical advantage from a method equivalent to a mandatory control.
