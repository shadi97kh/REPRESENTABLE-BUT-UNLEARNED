# Contribution and equally informed comparisons

The added result is model-specific: an explicit compact five-law identity, a joint four-coefficient projected selection with its empirical remainder, and a piecewise integration/precision argument for the resulting regular target statistic. It establishes a mathematical computational improvement relative to the saved implementation schedule, not a new general ML principle.

**STANDARD RESULT APPLIED.** Semiparametric inverse-functional estimation already distinguishes target recovery from nuisance recovery and characterizes the efficient influence through the adjoint score operator. This work constructs a particular, generally nonminimum-norm influence and estimator; it does not improve that general theory. [Trabs, Theorem 2.7](https://arxiv.org/html/1307.6610v2)

**STANDARD RESULT APPLIED.** Integrated quantile functionals and their differentiability are established. Kaji's Theorem 5.1 has a fixed integrator-bound condition; it does not automatically cover this growing set of point queries and growing tail weights. The current model-specific empirical remainder checks those operations separately. [Kaji, sections 4–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.07572)

**STANDARD RESULT APPLIED.** Gaussian quadrature and its derivative-based remainder, including the two-node rule, are established numerical analysis. Splitting at known nonsmooth points is not a new learning mechanism. The contribution here is the explicit knot count, derivative envelopes and complete-statistic error allocation for these particular composed empirical quantiles. [NIST DLMF, section 3.5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.5)

The exact distinction that survives is the **combined model-specific construction and work proof**, including finite empirical operations whose error vanishes faster than \(N^{-1/2}\). None of contraction, preconditioning, telescoping, quantile integration, Hellinger testing, or Gauss quadrature is renamed as a new method. Equally informed controls may use all of these improvements and need not reconstruct every nuisance component.

**OPEN:** publication novelty of the particular compact argument, practical superiority, a useful accuracy/runtime tradeoff, efficient inference, and substantive scientific value beyond this calibrated mathematical example. Biological siRNA validity would require actual intervention/replicate data, justified correspondence with the latent-law/private-anchor assumptions, measured target validation, and treatment of confounding and noise. No GNN or biological claim follows from these analytic fixtures.
