## Static dependency findings and chronology

1. **No additional fatal stochastic or order-in-N contradiction was identified in this bounded inspection.** This is not a new proof of all inherited claims. External review should concentrate on A §§A–D, their transfer to C/T, and the numerical assumptions in Q/S.
2. **Integer transport is not globally continuous off model.** AR §3 supplies the defect. The compact theorem only needs fixed-chart calibration arguments, explicitly verified in C. A globally stated moving-query theorem would remain unsupported; the brief does not state one.
3. **Exact cap classification is a material qualification.** Q's deterministic comparison requires promised capped input or supplied original gate classification. S gives a different encoded-gate theorem in probability. Combining Q's every-dataset objective with S's O(log N) precision would be unsupported.
4. **Old open statements are retained deliberately.** P P6 and D's global modulus concern broader claims. L §7 predates the attaining statistic. A resolves specified local attainment; T replaces the anchor and can change the influence; S supplies qualified arithmetic stability. None resolves the global Hellinger modulus or efficiency.
5. **CLT versus risk:** bounded clipping alone does not supply uniform integrability needed for O(1/N) MSE. The packet makes no finite-sample risk, efficiency or useful-sample-size inference.
6. **Pilot scope:** the later adversarial review identifies a finite inverse bracket in the literal learner and method-cost counters omitting some operations. These qualify earlier “implemented-form” and “fully charged” wording. They do not invalidate the saved prediction values, nor the separate later mathematical compact statistic. The brief does not promote those historical implementation claims.
7. **Efficiency reporting:** the finer maximum solve residual is \(7.23776861244768\times10^{-15}\); the coarse twelve-direction residual is \(9.540492121253299\times10^{-15}\). The [erratum](errata.md) records exact source pointers. No solve was rerun.
