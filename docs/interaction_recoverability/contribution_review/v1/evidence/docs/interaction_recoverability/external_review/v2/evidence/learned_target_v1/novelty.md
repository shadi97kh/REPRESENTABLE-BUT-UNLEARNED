## Closest exact algebraic equivalence

After freezing a finite score dictionary and M, the correction is exactly Tikhonov-regularized Riesz/one-step estimation. The adaptive direction is exactly a stabilized generalized Rayleigh maximizer, and appending it is residual-driven Galerkin enrichment. The random and full-bank controls expose whether the adaptive restriction helps beyond these established ingredients. The fixed richer method spans all directions the adaptive method can produce; adaptive selection can trade bias against variance/computation, but cannot identify a new direction outside that bank.

The learned component is genuine fitting of both nonlinear profiles, their four shifts, and data-dependent correction directions. Genuine learning is not by itself a new statistical result. The sharp proposed distinction would require an original-model finite-sample nonlinear contrast bound with learned operators and honest omitted-direction control, plus an implementable estimator attaining it. `theorem.md` identifies why that result has not been obtained.

## Audit boundaries

The earlier source downloads and unknown-profile analysis were inspected rather than recreated. This new search concentrated on the closest primary papers and their assumptions, not a broad novelty hunt. It does not establish that no other paper contains a relevant special case. Native IV, outcome-regression and PDE algorithms are marked inapplicable as direct benchmarks when they require observations absent here; their applicable score/Riesz, spectral and enrichment algebra is included as a control. No baseline is supplied hidden profiles, latent variables or target labels.
