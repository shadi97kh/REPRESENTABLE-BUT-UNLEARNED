The executed neural-profile/Riesz pilot has no graph. This is static source and saved-record inspection. No project module, checkpoint, model forward pass, training routine or scientific test was executed. Numerical results below are transcribed from existing records, not recomputed. The [dependency map](dependency_map.md) and manifest identify the exact preserved evidence; the [mathematical review](mathematical_review.md) supplies the theorem scope.

## 2. What the frozen neural-profile pilot fitted

The profile parameterization is
\[
g_j(x)=e^x\left[1+\frac{.1}{4}\sum_{r=1}^4 a_{jr}\{\tanh(w_{jr}x+b_{jr})-\tanh(b_{jr})\}\right].
\]
Amplitudes are projected to \([-1,1]\), slopes to \([.25,1]\), offsets to \([-3,3]\) and coefficients to \([.501,.999]\). This finite family cannot represent every member of the original analytic envelope. The basis-control family has 32 profile coefficients plus four shifts, also 36 parameters, but is distinct from the correction's 36-direction bank.

The [frozen configuration](evidence/runs/interaction_recoverability/learned-target-v1-20260912-164000/frozen_config.json) and [experiment.py](evidence/interaction_recoverability/learned_target/experiment.py) specify three disjoint cyclic roles: source fitting F, source selection C, and score evaluation E. The chosen fit/checkpoint and correction are frozen before that rotation's E observations are used. The three corrected estimates are averaged; their rotations are not independent datasets. Pooled-fit comparators use their declared final checkpoint. The loader exposes source arrays 0–4. A model-defined joint response can be evaluated for the plug-in functional, but no observed joint-response data or evaluator target labels enter source fitting.

The historical run contains 24 synthetic datasets (two truth families, \(\delta=.1,.03\), \(n=256,1024\), three repeats) and ten methods, hence 240 recorded method rows. The record supports executed fits, not merely an optimizer in source code: a preserved d000 fold log includes source losses at steps 100, 200 and 300 and a selected checkpoint. The pilot's separations are outside the tiny-separation interval of the later family theorem.

CRPS uses 64-node Gaussian integration in the historical implementation. Geometry uses a 36-direction bank, numerical quadrature and a numerical direction metric; a 96/192-node comparison is a diagnostic rather than a certified whole-class error bound. Correction solves \((G+\lambda M)c=b\) and adds source-weighted evaluation-score means to the plug-in target. Enrichment solves the finite generalized residual problem using \(G+\tau M\), with \(\tau=.001\). The selection grid is \(\lambda\in\{10^{-5},.001,.1\}\); its displacement-radius term uses a heuristic .02 constant. Computation of the full bank precedes adaptive enrichment, so fewer selected directions are not proof of reduced total cost.

The [saved summary](evidence/runs/interaction_recoverability/learned-target-v1-20260912-164000/evaluation/summary.json) reports adaptive MSE 0.47939382834477345 versus richest fixed MSE 0.4970740214880364, a **3.5569%** aggregate reduction against the frozen **10%** continuation screen. The retained decision is **incremental-known**. The small aggregate descriptive gain is neither positive novelty evidence nor a biological result; no confidence claim is added here.

Concrete implementation/theory limitations remain:

- The inverse uses 52 bisections in the fixed bracket \([-24,24]\), with an exception outside it. First-order implicit-gradient attachment divides by a detached derivative. Since the mathematical response laws have support \((0,\infty)\), the literal implementation is not a total estimator on every possible sample. The [pilot theorem's](evidence/docs/interaction_recoverability/learned_target_v1/theorem.md) global ideal-inverse moment argument does not resolve that exception without a specified fallback.
- Finite neural fitting, finite quadrature, ridge bias, full-direction residuals and nonlinear transfer lack the bounds needed for the full-profile theorem. A finite Galerkin identity is not an infinite score-closure certificate. The pilot's conditional coarse risk bound supplies no useful shrinking rate.
- Effective ranks differ because acceptance and rank thresholds differ: the retained audit reports adaptive final ranks 12/11/10 in 37/32/3 rotations, while the random comparator has rank 12 in all 72. This weakens a clean interpretation of the adaptive comparison.
- Component timing counters omit some target/correction and path-diagnostic work; broader dataset/ledger accounting includes it. No new cost advantage is inferred. The frozen records and their negative interpretation are preserved.

## 3. What the executed RNA GNN learned

For the P4 RNA path, [row_features / batched_forward](evidence/scmp/revision/p4_gpu_math.py) use **90 node features**: four target-window nucleotide indicators, the repeated flattened 21-nt guide one-hot vector (84 entries), normalized position and its square. Windows have 50 nodes. There is no resolved passenger chemistry, positional modification, dose or delivery input. The RNA grammar permits noncrossing allowed base pairs with minimum loop length three and wobble enabled. Backbone edges and self-loops are fixed. `FastPrior` assigns fixed \(.5/T\) pair weights, computes marginals via a NumPy inside/outside algorithm and samples structures by discrete backtraces; the prior is not learned.

`AnchorEncoder` produces a scalar node sum \(b\) and one coefficient \(a_e\) from symmetrized endpoint features per candidate pair. It cannot see the selected edge indicators or conditioning mask. `SignedResidualMPNN` computes
\[
H_1=\operatorname{ReLU}(A\,\mathrm{lin0}(X)),\quad
H_2=\operatorname{ReLU}(A\,\mathrm{lin1}(H_1)),\quad g=\mathrm{readout}\!\left(\sum_iH_{2i}\right).
\]
The RNA run uses hidden width 12. All linear weights and biases are trainable in their respective phase. No attention, degree normalization, batch normalization or per-layer independent edge indicators appear. The anchor's expected prediction is computed as \(b+a^T\mu\); the residual is averaged over sampled adjacency matrices. Gradients flow through the ensemble average to network weights, not through the graph prior or discrete draws.

The [training source](evidence/scmp/revision/p4_gpu_rna.py) and [configuration](evidence/configs/revision_p4_gpu_v1.yaml) use 1,130 conditional reporter rows, split 711/157/262 by the retained 13-mer component rule. Phase A trains the anchor for 20 epochs; phase B freezes it and trains a zero-initialized residual for 20 epochs. Adam uses learning rate .001, batch size 16 and eight graph draws per minibatch. The loss is the squared error between the **mean prediction over structures** and the observed assay label; it does not label each latent structure with that assay value. Development MSE selects \(\gamma=.125\) from \(\{0,.125,.25,.5,1\}\), with 64 evaluation draws.

[Training A](evidence/runs/revision/p4_gpu-20260911-171200-784773-rna_training_A.json) records 900 steps; [training B](evidence/runs/revision/p4_gpu-20260911-171200-784773-rna_training_B.json) ends at cumulative step 1,800. The [cycle record](evidence/runs/revision/p4_gpu-20260911-171200-784773-cycle.json) totals **2,400** optimizer steps including 600 in a separate capability task, and lists actual checkpoint paths/hashes. The report generator's `training_executed: false` describes report generation, not that earlier cycle. Checkpoints were not loaded in this review.

The controlling [final saved report](evidence/docs/p4_actual_model_executed-20260911-171200-784773-final.md) is **NO-GO FOR THIS SUBMISSION ROUTE**. RNA Spearman was approximately .2508 versus .6475 for the guide-only baseline, difference −.3966 with saved interval [−.5150, −.2584]; anchor-only was .2539. NDCG was .8320 versus .9132. The capability check and useful learned verification-gain check also failed. These are saved, exploratory conditional-context results after earlier P3 exposure, not an independent successful biological validation. Floating-point predictor bounds carry the retained `float_unverified` qualification and do not certify biological order.

Two name collisions must not obscure the scope: the GNN's “anchor” is an additive graph model component, not a calibrated component-private intervention; the checkpoint's “A_plus_B” means anchor plus residual, not two chemical edits A and B.

## 4. Proposed candidate ranking and the measured estimand

A prospective siRNA system would take the candidate **guide and passenger sequences**, resolved sugar/base/linkage chemistry at each position (including stereochemistry when relevant), target transcript/version and site, and recorded cell, dose, time, delivery and assay/readout context. Its proposed output is a ranked on-target activity shortlist plus predicted effects of named edits on that fixed background. This input/output specification is not an implemented theorem-covered system.

For a fixed sequence background, target/site and comparable assay, let edits A and B define four conditions and let \(\mu_{ab}\) be their expected measured outcome on **one declared endpoint scale**. Then
\[
I_{AB}=\mu_{11}-\mu_{10}-\mu_{01}+\mu_{00},\qquad
\mu_{11}=\mu_{10}+\mu_{01}-\mu_{00}+I_{AB}.
\]
The combined mean depends on both main effects and the interaction. A large positive interaction need not give high activity; moreover, the preferred sign depends on whether the endpoint increases with knockdown or with residual expression. Log transformation changes this contrast; a contrast of mean logs is not the log of a mean contrast.

| Mathematical law | Required experimental evidence | Status in the inspected siRNA evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0: \(g_1(Z)+g_2(h(Z))\) | Reference construct with resolved identity, one fixed assay context and independently sampled comparable replicates. | Reference-like assay rows exist, but no identified reference law in a verified six-condition calibration panel. |
| 1 / \(e_1\): shifts \((\alpha_1,\beta_1)\) | Same background with named edit A; loadings in the specified box under the same two-component response model. | Proposed mapping; compatible replicated A law and loading interpretation unverified. |
| 2 / \(e_2\): shifts \((\alpha_2,\beta_2)\) | Same background with named edit B; analogous context and model evidence. | Proposed mapping; compatible replicated B law unverified. |
| 3 / \(e_3\): shifts \((1,0)\) | Independently justified intervention that moves **only component 1 by a known unit**, with component label known. | Missing private-anchor calibration. An extra convenient siRNA does not establish this property. |
| 4 / \(e_4\): shifts \((0,1)\) | Independently justified component-2-only known unit intervention. | Missing private-anchor calibration. |
| Sixth condition, A+B: summed shifts | Same background, combined edit, independently measured joint outcomes, held out from the five-source estimator for evaluation of \(I_{AB}\) and \(\mu_{11}\). | No eligible held-out joint labels tied to the required five calibrated source laws found. This is not a claim that no public joint chemistry measurements exist anywhere. |

The theorem needs \(n\) independent observations from each of the **five** fixed laws, total \(5n\). A collection of different sequences, sites, doses or constructs is not independent replication of one law. Shared plates/controls, nested technical replicates and biological replicate identities need to be recorded; the exact independence design must then be justified or the theorem changed. The evaluation sample for the sixth condition is additional to \(N\), not silently counted among the five sources.

The mathematical laws have positive, unbounded support because the profiles are increasing with exponential lower derivative bounds and the Gaussian coordinate has full support. Actual percentage inhibition is conceptually constrained and measured with normalization/noise; the inspected Huesken labels include zero and values above one. Those facts do not verify the exact pushforward law. A declared transformation would change the target and must establish its own support/model assumptions. The Gaussian latent distribution and known warp are modeling assumptions, not measured facts in these records.

## 6. What each kind of contrast would establish

Four queries of a frozen predictor produce
\(\widehat f_{11}-\widehat f_{10}-\widehat f_{01}+\widehat f_{00}\), a contrast **of that predictor**. It can describe its behavior even when its biological predictions are wrong. Directly querying it at A+B supplies a model value for the missing action and does not instantiate the five-law missing-condition experiment.

A held-out measured factorial contrast estimates an assay-specific biological mean contrast under the recorded experimental design. Agreement between a five-source estimate and that held-out contrast could evaluate statistical extrapolation, provided the source assumptions and endpoint comparability were justified. That experiment has not been demonstrated here.

A causal contrast additionally needs suitable intervention assignment, consistency, comparable context, absence or control of confounding and interference, and an estimand appropriate to that design. A molecular mechanism requires further mechanistic evidence. Neither model contrasts nor the five-law statistical theorem alone supplies those claims.

Applicable controls would use the same information: simple sequence-and-chemistry predictors, matched token or tabular models, ordinary factorial/interaction models, and published predictors only where input definitions, endpoint scales and training provenance are compatible. For the five-law statistical problem, inverse, likelihood, confidence-set/functional and direct-target controls should have the same observed sources and known warp/anchors, with no hidden profiles, latent variables or joint outcomes. These are comparison requirements, not a proposal to launch training.

**Current application conclusion:** the repository demonstrates data/provenance auditing, ordinary predictor/model-explanation preparation and an executed but unsuccessful conditional RNA GNN comparison. For the combined interaction-recoverability theorem, siRNA is a **motivating application only**. Private calibration, comparable replicated response laws, response-model compatibility and eligible held-out joint labels remain missing or unverified. No measured siRNA chemistry interaction, causal benefit, mechanistic conclusion or successful theorem-covered candidate-ranking system is established.
