## Item 1 — closed% + timeout% exceeded 100%

**Cause: two overlapping flags were summed.** `gap_rel == 0` is a *tightness*
attainment flag; `timeout` is a *termination* flag. An instance can carry both —
the search reached zero gap but could not certify termination inside the matched
second. The report added them as if they partitioned the instances.

The record already contains a mutually exclusive field, `status`, and
`timeout` holds **if and only if** `status == "unresolved"` (verified across all
1,650 verifier records). So the corrected accounting needs no rerun.

| Arm | reported closed | reported timeout | sum | **corrected proved** | unresolved | sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| box | 99.1% | 0.9% | 100.0% | **99.1%** | 0.9% | 100.0% |
| intermediate_unconditional | 99.1% | 0.9% | 100.0% | **99.1%** | 0.9% | 100.0% |
| fixed_conditional | 83.6% | 22.1% | 105.8% | **77.9%** | 22.1% | 100.0% |
| learned_conditional | 74.8% | 33.9% | 108.8% | **66.1%** | 33.9% | 100.0% |
| terminal_only | 69.7% | 30.3% | 100.0% | **69.7%** | 30.3% | 100.0% |

The excess is exactly the double-counted instances: 19 for `fixed_conditional`
and 29 for `learned_conditional`, and `proved = gap_zero − overlap` holds as an
integer identity (asserted in `tests/revision/test_audit_report.py`).

**Consequence.** The corrected resolution rates are *lower* than reported —
77.9% and 66.1%, not 83.6% and 74.8%. The gap between conditioning and the box
baseline is **wider** than the run report stated, not narrower. Denominators:
1,650 verifier records over 5 arms × 330; 30 voids excluded before the split;
291 timeouts, all of them `unresolved`.

### 105.7% or 105.8%?

Both, and the difference is itself a reporting question. Summing the **displayed
1-dp** values gives 83.6 + 22.1 = **105.7%**. Recomputing from raw counts gives
276/330 + 73/330 = **105.7576%**, which displays as 105.8%. Learned: **108.7%**
displayed-sum, **108.7879%** exact. Both are recorded in the audit record; the
excess itself is identical either way.

### Denominators, recomputed from cells

The report quoted `n = 330` per arm without deriving it. From the record:

```
360 cells attempted  (family × instance × seed)
 −30 voids           (all layered_dag_path, "empty or singleton family")
=330 cells evaluated  × 5 arms = 1,650 verifier records
```

| Family | evaluated | void | attempted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| path_matching | 150 | 0 | 150 |

| rna_noncrossing | 60 | 0 | 60 |

Void cells are **disjoint** from evaluated cells (verified), so they are excluded
before the arms run and no arm is advantaged by their removal.

**The 291 timeouts are `(cell, arm)` records, not cells.** They are 291 of the
1,650 records, i.e. 17.6% of records — not 291 of 330 cells. The report's
execution paragraph listed 291 timeouts beside 330-row arms, inviting exactly
that misreading.

### First tolerance-attainment times: not instrumented

The plan asks for a first-attainment column. **It cannot be produced from
protocol-v1 records.** Each verifier record carries only:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| `wall_s` | time the arm **stopped** |
| `matched_wall_s` | the matched budget (1.0 s for every arm) |
| `timeout` | terminal flag |

There is no per-refinement timeline. `wall_s` is not a substitute: for a
timed-out record it is the budget itself, not the moment the gap first met
tolerance. Median `wall_s` (box 0.060 s, fixed 0.567 s, learned 0.632 s) is a
stopping time and must not be reported as a time-to-resolution.

**Protocol v2 requirement.** Log `(t, L, U, status)` at every bound update, and
derive first-attainment and the anytime gap AUC from that stream. This is a
prerequisite for the P2 comparison, not an optional extra.

### Negative gaps: recorded as a defect, not clipped

Scanning all arms for `gap < 0` (a bound inversion):

- verifier records: **0**
- `runs/policy_pilot/*.json`: **12** occurrences, minimum **−1.1369e−13**

The inversion appears once per arm in **both** policy-pilot records, including
`baseline_unconditional`, so it is a property of the shared float bound
evaluation rather than of conditioning. It is roundoff-scale and consistent with
`float_unverified` status. Per the plan it is **recorded, not clipped**, and an
enclosed implementation (P4) must make it impossible rather than small.

**Protocol v2 requirement.** One mutually exclusive termination status
(`proved | refuted | unresolved-timeout | unresolved-other | infeasible | error`),
with tolerance-attainment kept as a separate field carrying its own
first-attainment timestamp. Rates computed only from explicit denominators.

## Item 3 — ranking coverage 45.5% or 90.9%

**Cause: unordered numerator over an ordered denominator.** Both numbers are
arithmetically correct; they answer different questions.

`resolved_comparison_demo` iterates ordered pairs `(i, j), i ≠ j`, giving
`pairs = 12 × 11 = 132`, and counts strict inequalities `u_i < l_j`.

Since `l_i ≤ u_i` for every candidate, `u_i < l_j` and `u_j < l_i` cannot both
hold. Each resolved inequality therefore identifies a **distinct unordered
pair**, so the matched denominator is `12 × 11 / 2 = 66`:

> **60 / 66 = 90.9%** of candidate comparisons resolved, not 45.5%.

The reported figure understates coverage by exactly a factor of two. This is a
real-arithmetic argument, not a measurement.

**Unchanged:** this remains an engineering check on *declared* windows. It
certifies the model's own ordering (100% of resolved pairs). It is **not an RNA
result** and no measured agreement is computable for these windows.

## Item 4 — G3's direction, comparator, and the predictor's actual inputs

**Direction: correct and explicit in code.**

```
difference     = rho_unconstrained − rho_certifiable_gamma1
decision       = PASS iff upper 95% CI of the difference < margin (0.05)
observed       = −0.3361, CI [−0.4858, −0.1869]  →  PASS
```

**Comparator: not informative.** The gate's *constrained* arm is `gamma1` (A+B,
mean 0.4573), not the best available certifiable model `gamma0` (A, mean
0.7253). The free arm scored 0.1212. So G3 asks whether A+B is noninferior to a
model that lost badly, and never compares the free model against the strongest
constrained one. The PASS is arithmetically right and carries no weight.

**Graph inputs traced.** `experiments/_huesken.py:build_graphs` folds each
**21-mer guide sequence's own MFE structure** (`bpp_lattice(s)`); no target
window is constructed anywhere in the predictor path. This resolves the apparent
contradiction between a trained A/A+B table and blocked window arms: these arms
never needed a window.

Two consequences, and they point in opposite directions:

- **Favourable.** No native transcript was substituted for a reporter construct
  here. Unlike F9, the folded sequence *is* the assayed molecule. The optimisation
  comparison is legitimate as a diagnostic.
- **Limiting.** The graph is the guide's intramolecular fold, a feature of the
  guide — not target structure. These numbers cannot support any
  target-structure claim, and the single held-out gene (`UBE2D3`, test n = 69 of
  400) cannot support a population claim.

## Item 5 — "non-negative additive anchor"

**Refuted against the code.** `AnchorEncoder.a_out` is `nn.Linear(d_hid, 1)` with
unconstrained weight and bias; no clamp, softplus, abs or `nonneg` flag appears
in `AnchorEncoder` or `SignedResidualMPNN`. Negative parameters are present at
initialisation.

The non-negativity constraint belongs to **certmp's monotone MPNN (phase one)**.
The report's limits table carried it across to phase two, where it does not
apply.

This matters for design: a signed anchor is strictly more expressive, and signed
coefficients do **not** obstruct the support oracle, which maximises a linear
objective over the family regardless of coefficient sign.
