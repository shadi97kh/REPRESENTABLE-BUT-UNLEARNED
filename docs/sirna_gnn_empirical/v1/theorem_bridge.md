# No transferred five-law guarantee

The implemented GNN consumes sequence, positional chemistry and available context and minimizes supervised loss on measured inhibition. Its training data are pooled published molecule/assay means, with incomplete replicate and normalization metadata outside the primary-reconciled APP cohort. At inference it predicts a scalar and scores a pair by subtraction. It does not compute empirical response quantiles, identify the four coefficients, invert the commutator or use calibrated private anchors.

The preserved five-law theorem concerns five independent outcome strata on one fixed background, n observations each (N=5n), a known warp, two calibrated private-unit actions, a specified latent law and unknown analytic monotone profiles. Pointwise attainment, efficient variance and finite-sample minimax risk have distinct quantifiers. None supplies a guarantee for this graph regressor. The theorem's fixed/disclosable hard profiles do not isolate the cost of learning an unknown profile.

Private anchors are not prerequisites for ordinary B1 prediction, B2 observed chemical differences or B3 four-condition measured contrasts. They are additional conditions for a five-law applicability claim, which is not made. Reported means/SDs are not independent stratum samples. Assay-scale compatibility, private-anchor calibration and Gaussian/profile assumptions are unverified. Backend no-go and the open historical reference-efficiency question remain unchanged in the preserved theoretical manuscript. No theorem, efficient estimator, causal mechanism or new wet-lab validation is claimed for this campaign.
