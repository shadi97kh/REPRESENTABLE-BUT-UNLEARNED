## Exact reduction and decision

For n unique raw states, represent the unknown response by mu in R^n. Observations are linear inequalities in mu. Encoding equivalences with uniform residual eta become signed pairwise inequalities. The query is c^T mu. Thus the entire inference proposal is a bounded linear program, or an interval hull of finitely many such programs. Its exact reference enumerates vertices; the standard LP theorem supplies correctness. Appending a conventional learned graph encoder does not change that reduction or justify response-class membership. Four evaluations and a pair/contrast loss do not introduce a new architectural component.

G1 is FAIL for this candidate: there is no identified algorithmic or theoretical advance beyond that reduction. This is stronger evidence than failure to find an identical title, and narrower than claiming that no new method could ever exist. G2 remains UNRESOLVED for the desired adaptive learned guarantee. G3 is blocked and unexecuted.
