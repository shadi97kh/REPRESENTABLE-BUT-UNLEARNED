# Falsifiable candidate and strongest supported contribution

Candidate claim tested: adding representation-aware compatible-response inference to the trained graph encoder supplies a new efficient algorithm with valid interaction uncertainty despite missing joint support and learned representation loss, beyond standard optimal recovery under the same information and assumptions.

Decision: not established. The implemented finite-state addition is standard linear optimization. Its exact result is conditional on externally justified response-class membership and is exponential in the reference implementation. It supplies neither an adaptive end-to-end guarantee nor demonstrated predictive benefit. No new training campaign or positive pilot was run after this failure.

The strongest supported paper contribution remains an empirical diagnostic: matched support/message controls, source-dependent calibration and provenance analysis, and measured B2/B3 evaluations clarify the limitations of a conventional chemistry-aware siRNA predictor. Conditional S7 improvements over no-message persist in two sensitivities, while the strongest observed baselines still lead and measured sequence-specific chemistry prediction is not established. This is retrospective measured-data evaluation, not independent new-study or wet-lab validation.

New work here: the explicit reduction, executable exact-reference/counterexample checks, independent saved-result/dependency verification, gate decisions and vector workflow. These are completed investigation artifacts, not a claimed new ML method. The historical five-law proofs and backend no-go remain separate and unchanged.
