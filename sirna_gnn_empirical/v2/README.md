This is the executed targeted follow-up. The completed v1 campaign is read-only input.

Run `bash sirna_gnn_empirical/v2/run_all.sh` to verify completed stages and resume unfinished ones. The scientific stages are audit, source_checks, plan/profile, factorial, grouped, b2, external, external_uncertainty, evaluate and figures. The paper and integrity stages are administrative and require the manuscript/full workspace. Every scientific fit uses a bounded predeclared protocol. New APP results are retrospective; B2 supervision is an explicitly new within-APP grouped task. Davis S7 is separately sourced, qualified native-outcome assessment. S1 remains quarantined.

`core.py` specifies the supported model and fitting behavior. Protocol and source hashes, all fits/checkpoints, optimizer histories and stage logs live in the timestamped run identified by `active_run.txt`. A completed stage verifies its saved output hashes without retraining. A completed fit returns stored predictions; interrupted neural fits resume their last full-epoch state. Up to one incomplete epoch of work may need repeating, and this campaign reports that count uncertainty honestly.

Manuscript authoring scripts are administrative. `prepare_paper.py` and `paper_tables.py` were initial scaffolding commands; DO NOT rerun them on accepted edited prose. `paper.py` compiles existing manuscript v3 without regenerating its text. `deliver.py` refreshes documentation/archives from completed artifacts. No backend, historical allowance wrapper, wet-lab work, publication or external contact is part of this pipeline.
